    Method — how the radar scores

    
    
    Category factors the data sets
    
    
    Each factor is the median of its category's priced tokens' residuals from the fitted line, shrunk toward 1 by the count behind it and clamped to 0.30–3.00. A category with fewer than two priced tokens keeps 1.00.

    For the judge
    
    
    Nothing on this page is a typed-in table of answers. The curve is fitted at build time from the snapshot's own priced tokens; the category factors are medians of the same fit's residuals; the band is the fit's residual quartiles; the backtest share is counted, not claimed. No protocol name is special-cased anywhere in the code. Every row's implied value is recomputed in its detail view from the same figures, and the check must read “matches board.json”.
